    Dijkstra step by step

    Min-heap (distance, node), top first

    1. Dijkstra

    O((V + E) log V)

    A min-heap always pops the closest unsettled node (the greedy choice). Each open lane out of it is relaxed. Heap entries with an outdated, larger distance are skipped (lazy deletion). The nearest free bay of the right type wins, and the route is rebuilt from parent[].

    2. Prim's MST

    O(E log V)

    The tree grows from Gate A by always adding the cheapest lane that reaches a new node. The result is the least total road that still connects all 21 nodes, using V − 1 = 20 lanes.

    3. Lane closures

    dynamic graph

    Closing a lane flips one flag. Both directions share it because adj[u] stores the lane index. The next Dijkstra or Prim run skips it, so routes and the MST adapt, and unreachable bays are reported.
